## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Anti-acne / clears breakouts | For skin prone to blemishes — treatment claims turn a cosmetic into a drug |
| Strong whitening | Refines and evens skin texture |

## Notes

**This product needs a warning label in your content, and saying so will help you, not hurt you.**

Retinol and sun exposure do not mix. Users should avoid strong sun on treated areas and use sunscreen. Retinol is also generally avoided in pregnancy — a real consideration given your customer base. And "anti-acne" is a treatment claim: in most markets that language turns a cosmetic into a regulated drug.

Being the brand that says *"here's who should not use this"* is worth more than any claim you could make. Nobody else in this category does it.
