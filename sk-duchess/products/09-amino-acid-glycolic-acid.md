## What it is

The most modern formulation in the range — chemical rather than physical exfoliation. Appeals to a customer who already knows what an AHA is.

## Key ingredients

- **Glycolic acid** — An AHA; the smallest molecule in the class, so the most penetrating.
- **Amino acids** — Mild, conditioning surfactant system.

## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Brightening | Smooths and refines skin texture |

## Notes

**This is the SKU that reaches a more informed buyer.** Someone searching "glycolic acid body wash" is a different, usually higher-value customer than someone searching "whitening soap". The brand is not currently talking to them.

Same guidance as retinol: glycolic acid increases sun sensitivity. Say so.

## Content angles

- "Physical scrub or chemical exfoliant?" — explains your own range and educates at once.
- Keratosis pilaris ("chicken skin" on arms) is exactly what a glycolic body wash is for, and it is a heavily searched, under-served problem.
