## What it is

A scrub-format wash with visible exfoliating particles. Turmeric gives it a distinctive colour and a strong cultural resonance in the Nigerian market, where turmeric is already trusted for skin.

## Key ingredients

- **Vitamin C** — Brightening story.
- **Turmeric extract** — Antioxidant and soothing; strong local credibility.
- **Hydrolysed collagen** — Firmness and suppleness.

## Claims currently in the market

Taken from retailer listings and the label. These are not necessarily claims you should repeat:

- Brightening and firming
- For dry, oily and troubled skin
- Perfect for cracked, dry skin
- SPF 30 (on some labels)

## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Brightening and firming | Polishes and smooths, leaving skin looking bright |
| For troubled skin | Suits most skin types — avoid implying treatment of a condition |

## Notes

**Turmeric is the most locally credible ingredient in your entire range.** Nigerian customers already believe in it. That belief is earned and free — lean on it much harder than the brand currently does.

Usage guidance matters for scrubs: over-exfoliation is the most common way a customer gets a bad result and blames the product. Tell people two or three times a week, not daily. That honesty builds more trust than a claim ever will.

## Content angles

- "How often should you actually use a scrub?" — the honest answer wins trust.
- Turmeric's place in Nigerian skincare tradition, tied to the product.
- Texture content: scrub particles in close-up perform extremely well on TikTok.
