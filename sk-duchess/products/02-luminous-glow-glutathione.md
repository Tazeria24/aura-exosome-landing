## What it is

Sits very close to the 13-in-1 and is frequently confused with it by both customers and retailers. Marketed on glow and luminosity rather than the multi-benefit stack.

## Key ingredients

- **L-Glutathione** — Primary active.
- **Vitamin C** — Antioxidant support.
- **Natural fruit extracts** — Fragrance and marketing story.

## Claims currently in the market

Taken from retailer listings and the label. These are not necessarily claims you should repeat:

- Luminous glow
- Brightens dull skin
- Fades dark spots
- Even, luminous tone

## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Brightens dull skin | Revives dull-looking skin |
| Fades dark spots | Helps even the look of dark spots over time |

## Notes

**This variant and the 13-in-1 are the brand's biggest merchandising problem.** Retailers list them interchangeably, sometimes at a ₦4,000 spread, and customers cannot tell them apart from a thumbnail. Clear differentiation — or consolidating them — would recover real margin.
