## What it is

A berry-scented scrub combining the glutathione story with kojic acid. Positioned at the strongest end of the tone-evening range.

## Key ingredients

- **Gluta berry / L-glutathione** — Primary active.
- **Vitamin C** — Antioxidant.
- **Kojic acid** — Tyrosinase inhibitor; the most functionally active tone ingredient in the range.
- **Collagen** — Firmness story.
- **Scrub particles** — Physical exfoliation.

## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Extra whitening | Evens and refines tone |
| Reduces acne scars | Helps soften the look of marks left behind |

## Notes

**Highest regulatory exposure in the range.** Kojic acid plus "extra whitening" plus acne-scar claims is the combination most likely to be rejected by ad review or flagged by a regulator. Kojic acid is restricted or capped in several markets.

Keep this one organic-only. Do not put spend behind it, and be careful about before-and-after imagery, which is treated as a claim in its own right.
