## What it is

A creamy milk-based scrub adding arbutin to the glutathione base. The milk format gives it a softer, more comforting positioning than the berry scrub.

## Key ingredients

- **Milk protein** — Conditioning; the creamy character.
- **L-Glutathione** — Primary active.
- **Arbutin** — A gentler tone-evening agent than hydroquinone; well regarded.
- **Scrub particles** — Physical exfoliation.

## Claims currently in the market

- Extra whitening

## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Extra whitening | Softens and evens the look of the skin |

## Notes

**Arbutin is a better story than the marketing tells.** It is widely regarded as one of the gentler tone-evening ingredients, and "gentle" is a genuine differentiator in a category full of harsh products. Confirm with the factory whether it is alpha or beta arbutin and at what concentration — that specificity is exactly what a serious skincare audience responds to.
