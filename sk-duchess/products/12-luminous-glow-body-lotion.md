## Ad-safe rewrites

Meta and TikTok both restrict paid promotion of skin bleaching, and treatment claims can reclassify a cosmetic as a drug. Left column is what the market says; right column is what you can safely say.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| 7 days | Remove this entirely — a specific timeframe is the single most dangerous claim you carry |
| Stimulates collagen production | Leaves skin feeling firmer and more supple |
| Premium skin whitening | For visibly even, radiant skin |

## Notes

**"7 Days" is the riskiest claim in the entire catalogue.** A named timeframe is a specific, testable, falsifiable promise. It invites regulatory action, it invites refund demands, and it invites a viral "I used it for 7 days and nothing happened" video — which is the exact format TikTok rewards most. Every unhappy customer gets a free, well-structured attack on your brand.

Remove it. Replace with honest guidance: results build gradually with consistent use.

**Strategically, this is the product that matters most.** A leave-on lotion stays on the skin, so it does more than a rinse-off gel ever can — and it is the natural repeat purchase. Bundling lotion with gel is the clearest route to raising order value.

Everything above was assembled from third-party retailer listings, not from SK Duchess. Treat it as a draft to correct, not a source of truth.

- [ ] Exact product name as printed on the label

- [ ] NAFDAC registration number
- [ ] Batch code format and where it is printed
- [ ] Manufacture and expiry date conventions
